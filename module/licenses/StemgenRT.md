# StemgenRT vocal-separation weights

The bundled models are derived from the released StemgenRT vocals ONNX model by Axel Delafosse. DolbySoundMod extracts its backbone and splits the recurrent core and spectral-mask decoder into separate graphs.

Training dataset credits go to Z. Rafii et al. for [MUSDB18-HQ](https://zenodo.org/records/3338373), and to I. Pereira, F. Araújo, F. Korzeniowski and R. Vogl for [MoisesDB](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15913).
